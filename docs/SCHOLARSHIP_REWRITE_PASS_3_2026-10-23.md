## Yangi topilmalar

1. **UChicago va CUHK** — ikkalasida ham `degree_levels` da **Master va PhD** ko'rsatilgan,
   lekin ikkalasi ham **faqat bakalavr** dasturi. O'zbek magistranti behuda qaraydi.
   Ikkalasi ham `["Bachelor"]` ga tuzatildi.
2. **NTU** — "NTU Undergraduate Scholarship" degan umumiy nom ostida o'zbek talabasiga
   tegishli aniq dastur yo'q edi. Endi **Science & Technology Undergraduate Scholarship** —
   NTU sahifasida *"awarded to outstanding applicants from Asian countries or regions
   (except Singapore)"* deb yozilgan, ya'ni O'zbekiston kiradi.
3. **Fudan** — CSC (davlat) va Shanghai Government Scholarship (shahar) **bitta yozuvga**
   qo'shib yuborilgan edi. Ikkalasi butunlay boshqa dastur, boshqa muddat bilan.
4. **Peking/Fudan CSC** — $21 000 degan summa sahifada yo'q. Rasmiy konsullik e'lonida
   oylik stipendiya CNY 2 500 / 3 000 / 3 500 deb aniq yozilgan.

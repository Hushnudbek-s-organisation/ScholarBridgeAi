## Universitetlar bo‘yicha ma’lumot

### #10 — California Institute of Technology (Caltech)

**Profil va dasturlar.** Pasadena shahridagi xususiy, fan va muhandislikka kuchli yo‘naltirilgan institut. Rasmiy katalogda olti akademik bo‘linma bo‘ylab **26 bakalavr yo‘nalishi (“options”) va 17 minor** ko‘rsatilgan; misollar — computer science, chemical engineering, electrical engineering, mechanical engineering, physics, biology va bioengineering. Bakalavr tadqiqotining asosiy yo‘llaridan biri — yozgi SURF loyihalari. [1](https://www.catalog.caltech.edu/current/general-information/education-and-research/)

**Qabul.** Birinchi kursga topshiruvchilar SAT yoki ACT topshirishi kerak; Caltech kompozit balldan ko‘ra alohida bo‘limlarni ko‘radi va e’lon qilingan qat’iy minimal ball yo‘q. Kuchli matematik tayyorgarlik, hisob, fizika va kimyo muhim. Xalqaro talabalar uchun ingliz tili imtihoni odatda talab qilinadi, ayrim ingliz tilida ta’lim olgan yoki ingliz tili ona tili bo‘lgan abituriyentlar bundan mustasno. [1](https://www.admissions.caltech.edu/apply/first-year-applicants/standardized-tests) [4](https://www.admissions.caltech.edu/apply/first-year-applicants/international-applicants)

**2026–27 narxi (USD, bakalavriat).** Yillik tuition **$68,574**, majburiy fees **$2,655**; kampusda turar joy **$12,711**, ovqat **$9,285**. Bevosita xarajatlar **$93,225**, kitob/materiallar va shaxsiy xarajatlar bilan e’lon qilingan 9 oylik COA **$98,622**. Caltech Student Health Insurance Plan yillik **$5,388** bo‘lib, COA jadvaliga kiritilmagan; yangi talabalar uchun bir martalik **$600 Orientation Fee** ham qo‘shiladi. Taqqoslashda shu ikki qo‘shimcha to‘lovni alohida hisoblang. [1](https://www.catalog.caltech.edu/current/information-for-undergraduate-students/undergraduate-expenses/)

**Stipendiya va yordam.** Caltech merit-based stipendiya bermaydi; yordam moliyaviy ehtiyoj asosida. Xalqaro abituriyentlar uchun qabul need-aware/need-sensitive, ammo qabul qilingan talabaning isbotlangan ehtiyoji to‘liq qoplanishi maqsad qilinadi. Xalqaro talaba yordam kerakligini arizada ko‘rsatishi zarur; rasmiy siyosat yordam bilan topshirayotgan xalqaro abituriyentga Regular Decision yo‘lini tavsiya qiladi va birinchi yili yordam olmagan talaba keyingi yillarda odatda yangi yordam so‘ray olmaydi. [1](https://www.admissions.caltech.edu/apply/first-year-applicants/international-applicants) [3](https://admissions.caltech.edu/content/international-citizens)

### #11 — The University of Hong Kong (HKU)

**Profil va dasturlar.** Gonkongdagi davlat universiteti. Rasmiy bakalavr katalogi hozir arxitektura, san’at, biznes, stomatologiya, ta’lim, muhandislik, huquq, tibbiyot, fan va ijtimoiy fanlar bilan birga computing/data science, biomedical engineering, innovation, qo‘shma va double-degree dasturlarini ham ko‘rsatadi; qidiruv sahifasida 152 ta mos natija bor. Dastur va fakultetni alohida tekshirish kerak. [1](https://admissions.hku.hk/programmes/undergraduate-programmes)

**Qabul.** Xalqaro/Non-JUPAS abituriyentlari o‘z mamlakatidagi maktab attestati yoki xalqaro malaka bo‘yicha topshiradi; fan talablari tanlangan darajaga bog‘liq. IELTS Academic uchun umumiy minimum **6.5**, Writing va Speaking bo‘limlarining har biri kamida **6.0** bo‘lishi kerak. 2026-yil 21-yanvardan keyingi TOEFL iBT yangi shkalasida minimum 5.0 overall va Writing/Speaking’da 5.0 talab qilinadi. [1](https://admissions.hku.hk/apply/international-qualifications/english-language-requirement) [3](https://admissions.hku.hk/sites/default/files/2025-09/General%20Information%20NJ%202026.pdf)

**2026–27 narxi (HKD/yil, bakalavriat).** Mahalliy talabalar uchun yillik composition fee **HK$47,000**. Xalqaro/non-local talabalar uchun non-STEM fakultetlari **HK$224,000**, STEM fakultetlari/maktablari **HK$249,000**; STEM stavkasiga STEM fee kiritilgan. Bu turar joy va yashash xarajatlari emas, yillik universitet to‘lovi. [3](https://admissions.hku.hk/sites/default/files/2025-09/General%20Information%20NJ%202026.pdf)

**Stipendiya.** Barcha abituriyentlar avtomatik ko‘rib chiqiladi; alohida ariza talab qilinmaydi. Eng yuqori reytingdagi nomzodlar uchun entrance scholarship to‘liq tuition waiver bilan birga turar joy stipendiyasi va yillik yashash puli qiymatigacha yetishi mumkin. O‘qishga kirgandan so‘ng ariza topshiriladigan boshqa merit stipendiyalari ham bor. [3](https://admissions.hku.hk/sites/default/files/2025-09/General%20Information%20NJ%202026.pdf)

**Profil va dasturlar.** Singapurdagi davlat tadqiqot universiteti. 2026-yilgi rasmiy darajalar ro‘yxatida accountancy/business, engineering, computing, data science/AI, biological sciences, medicine, communication, economics, humanities va ijtimoiy fanlar bor; single, double-degree va second-major yo‘llari ham mavjud. [1](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/education/degree-programmes)

**Qabul.** Xalqaro abituriyentning maktab malakasi (masalan, IB, A-level yoki mamlakat milliy attestati), kerakli fanlari va qo‘shimcha tanlov bosqichlari tanlangan dasturga qarab farqlanadi; ayrim yo‘nalishlarda suhbat yoki maxsus imtihon bo‘ladi. Ingliz tili secondary school’da ta’lim tili bo‘lmagan yoki ingliz tilini ikkinchi til sifatida olgan ayrim malakalar uchun alohida English score talab qilinadi. AY2027 xalqaro malakalar uchun ariza davri **2026-yil 15-oktabrda** ochiladi; mamlakat va malakaga qarab muddatlar farq qiladi. [1](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/admissions/undergraduate/admission-guide/international-qualifications) [2](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/docs/default-source/undergraduate-admissions/msr/emsr_intnl.pdf?sfvrsn=35f52b2c_4) [3](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/admissions/undergraduate/admissions)

**2026 narxi (SGD/yil, full-time bakalavriat).** MOE Tuition Grant bilan beriladigan “boshqa dasturlar” asosiy qatorida ASEAN xalqaro talabalari uchun **S$18,050**, boshqa xalqaro talabalar uchun **S$21,400**. Grantni olmaydigan xalqaro talabalarning umumiy qatori lab-based dasturlarda **S$40,600**, non-lab dasturlarda **S$36,350**. Dastur istisnolari bor: masalan, Medicine grant bilan ASEAN xalqaro talabalari uchun **S$80,850**, boshqa xalqaro talabalar uchun **S$91,250**. Xalqaro talabalar uchun e’lon qilingan bu summalar GST’ni o‘z ichiga oladi. [3](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/admissions/undergraduate/fees-and-funding/tuition-fees/accepted-programme-offer-in-2026)

**Grant va stipendiya.** MOE Tuition Grant olgan Singapur fuqarosi bo‘lmagan talabalar bitirgandan keyin Singapurdagi tashkilotda **3 yil ishlash majburiyatini** oladi. Nanyang Global Scholarship barcha millatlarga ochiq va grantdan keyingi subsidiyalangan tuition’ni qoplashi, shuningdek yiliga **S$6,500 yashash puli**, **S$2,000 gacha turar joy yordami**, shartlarga muvofiq **S$8,000 gacha safar granti** va bir martalik **S$2,000 kompyuter yordami** berishi mumkin. Scholarshipning o‘zidan tashqari MOE grant bond’i xalqaro talabalar uchun baribir amal qiladi. [5](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/admissions/undergraduate/financial-matters/tuition-grants) [2](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/admissions/undergraduate/scholarships/scholarship-opportunities/detail/nanyang-scholarship)

**Profil va dasturlar.** Chicago shahridagi xususiy universitet. Bakalavr kolleji majburiy Core curriculum’ni major bilan birga olib boradi. 2026–27 katalogidagi misollar: economics, computer science, data science, biology, physics, mathematics, public policy, history va anthropology. [1](http://collegecatalog.uchicago.edu/thecollege/programsofstudy/) [2](http://collegecatalog.uchicago.edu/thecollege/curriculum/)

**Qabul.** SAT/ACT test-optional; universitet “No Harm” siyosatini ham ta’riflaydi. Birinchi kursga Common App yoki Coalition Application, UChicago supplement va maktab hujjatlari bilan topshiriladi. [6](https://collegeadmissions.uchicago.edu/apply/uchicago-admissions-faq/)

**2026–27 narxi (USD).** Tuition **$75,960**; Student Services Fee **$1,677**, UPass **$318**. Kampusda housing/food **$21,414**, kitob **$1,800**, shaxsiy xarajat **$1,950**, safar allowance’i **$702**. E’lon qilingan on-campus COA jami **$103,821**. Bu summaga health insurance kirmaydi; 2026-yilda birinchi marta kirganlar birinchi va ikkinchi yilda alohida **$720 Class Fee** to‘laydi. [1](https://financialaid.uchicago.edu/undergraduate/how-aid-works/undergraduate-costs/)

**Yordam va stipendiya.** UChicago qabul qilingan xalqaro talabaning ko‘rsatilgan ehtiyojini 100% qoplashini, grant-asosli va loan expectation’siz paket berishini bildiradi. Birinchi kurs xalqaro abituriyentlari mahalliy abituriyentlar bilan bir xil contextual review’dan o‘tadi, biroq o‘qish davomida istalgan paytda need-based aid kerak bo‘lishi mumkin deb hisoblasa, moliyaviy hujjatlarni qabul qaroridan oldin topshirishi shart; birinchi kursda yordam so‘ramagan xalqaro talaba keyingi yilda yangidan ariza bera olmaydi. Birinchi kursga topshirganlar merit stipendiyalari uchun avtomatik ko‘rib chiqiladi. [1](https://collegeadmissions.uchicago.edu/apply/application/optional-materials/) [2](https://collegeadmissions.uchicago.edu/financial-support/financial-support-faq/) [1](https://collegeadmissions.uchicago.edu/financial-overivew/)

**2027-yil kuzidan qo‘shimcha siyosat.** UChicago 2027-yil Autumn Quarter’dan boshlab odatiy aktivlarga ega, yillik daromadi $250,000 dan past oilalarga tuition’ni; $125,000 dan past oilalarga tuition, fees, housing va meals’ni qoplashini e’lon qilgan. Xalqaro oilalar uchun daromad/aktivlarni hisoblash tartibini alohida moliyaviy yordam bo‘limidan tasdiqlash kerak. [1](https://www.isd.pku.edu.cn/cn/news/detail.php?id=800) [1](https://www.isd.pku.edu.cn/cn/news/detail.php?id=106) [1](https://oic.bjmu.edu.cn/English/International_Students/Admission/79194c881e8b42619aadc893ee39609d.htm)

## Xulosa va tekshirish uchun eslatmalar

1. **Faqat QS o‘rniga qarab tanlamang.** 2026 reytingi umumiy institutsional o‘lchov; kerakli major, litsenziya/akkreditatsiya, til, amaliyot va keyingi ish/o‘qish rejasini alohida solishtiring.

Transparent Admissions Intelligence

Fit and chance are not the same thing.

A university can be a great fit without being easy to get into. ScholarBridge keeps those concepts separate and explains the evidence behind an estimate.

  • University Match measures profile compatibility.
  • Admission estimates use available outcome evidence and clearly identify their data basis.
  • As ScholarBridge collects consented application outcomes, the evidence layer becomes richer over time.

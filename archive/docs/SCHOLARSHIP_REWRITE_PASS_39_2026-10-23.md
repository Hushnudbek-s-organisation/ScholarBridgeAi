**a) Eurydice (Yevropa Komissiyasi) tasdiqlaydi:** «International students are **not included** in the loans from Lånekassen, apart from Ukrainian refugees and students that have self-financed 3 years study in Norway.» Ya'ni O'zbekistonlik talaba uchun bu yo'l **yopiq**.

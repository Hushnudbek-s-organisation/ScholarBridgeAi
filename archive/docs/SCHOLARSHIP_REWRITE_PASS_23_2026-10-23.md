## 2. Qolgan to'rtta qator

### 811 — IIUM Postgraduate Ummatic Scholarship
Eski yozuv: *"IIUM Ummatic Scholarship & MIS"*, $4 200, "May–Jun / Nov–Dec".

**Ummatic nomi ostida IKKI xil grant bor:**
- **Bakalavriat Ummatic** — **yangi abituriyentga emas**: 2-kurs va undan yuqori, **ro'yxatdan o'tgan** talaba bo'lishi shart.
- **Magistratura/PhD Ummatic** — O'zbekistondan kelgan abituriyent ishlatishi mumkin bo'lgani shu:
  - **to'liq kontrakt — MYR 81 400 gacha** (6 semestr, Takaful, baholash, bench to'lovlari bilan)
  - **MYR 1 000/oy** — 36 oygacha (**MYR 36 000** gacha)
  - CGPA **3.70 ikkala darajada ham**, yosh **30** gacha, IELTS **6.5**, publikatsiya
  - **3 ta markaz** (Islom banki, Stomatologiya, Farmatsiya) — faqat kontrakt, stipendiyasiz

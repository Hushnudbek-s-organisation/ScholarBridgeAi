Where do I find the address?
  1. Open the ScholarBridge app on your laptop.
  2. Choose Share → Allow phone on this Wi-Fi.
  3. Scan the QR code with this phone, or type the address it shows (for example 192.168.1.42:4310).
  4. The phone and the laptop must be on the same Wi-Fi network.

Using a hosted ScholarBridge instead? Enter its address, e.g. https://scholarbridge.example.com.
